Deep tissue vs Swedish massage

The useful distinction is not which style is “better.” It is which style matches the outcome, pressure, and pacing you want from the session.

Deep tissue

  • Usually firmer and more targeted.
  • Often centered on specific muscle groups.
  • Fits requests that describe persistent tightness or concentrated recovery work.
  • Benefits from clear communication about pressure.
Read the deep-tissue page →

Swedish

  • Usually broader and more flowing.
  • Often chosen for general relaxation and decompression.
  • May fit when there is no single problem area.
  • Can still vary from light to moderate pressure.

Choose deep tissue when

You can name the body area, prefer focused pressure, and want the session organized around a specific recovery or tension goal.

Choose Swedish when

You want broad tension relief, slower pacing, and a session designed around general relaxation rather than concentrated muscle work.

Choose sports recovery when

The request is tied to a workout, event, training block, repetitive activity, or recovery schedule. Sports recovery is defined more by context and goal than by one fixed technique.
