Training, mobility, and recovery

Sports recovery massage in Manhattan and Harlem

Sports recovery sessions are organized around what your body has recently done: lifting, running, cycling, travel, repetitive work, or a demanding training block. The request should describe the activity and the recovery goal.

Request sports-recovery availability

Three useful booking contexts

After training

Describe the workout, areas carrying fatigue, and how soon the next training session occurs.

Between events

Share the performance schedule so the session can prioritize recovery without overworking the area.

Mobility maintenance

Explain the motion or position that feels restricted and whether the goal is comfort, range, or maintenance.

What the intake system evaluates

The pipeline uses timing, location, budget, contact completeness, and detail quality to prioritize the request. This is not a medical diagnosis. It is a routing mechanism that helps distinguish an immediate booking request from a general inquiry.

Prepare before submitting

  • Name the sport, workout, or repetitive activity.
  • List the main body areas you want addressed.
  • Give a realistic availability window.
  • Mention whether you prefer firm or moderate pressure.
