Before you request

Useful answers, without the fine-print fog.

The form is intentionally a request flow. Final availability and session details are confirmed afterward.

Does submitting the form confirm an appointment?

No. It sends a structured request and returns a receipt. The next action shown with the receipt explains what happens after submission.

Is payment collected here?

No payment field is included in this intake. Budget helps qualify the request, but submitting the form does not charge you.

What information should I include?

Location, timing, the service that feels closest, and any useful context such as pressure preference, focus areas, or accessibility needs.

Why does the receipt matter?

It gives the request a traceable identifier and confirms that the active host accepted the submission.
